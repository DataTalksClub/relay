What do these numbers mean?

Sent counts recipients currently in the Sent state; later bounces, complaints, or unsubscribes can reduce it. Delivered counts recorded delivery confirmations, which remain after later events. Delivery confirmation means the receiving server accepted the email, not that it reached the inbox.

Unique opens and clicks count recipients with recorded tracking events. Tracked opens are imperfect: privacy protections and automated image loading can create or hide opens. Clicks can include automated scanners.

Queued means waiting for processing. Processed counts recipients with a terminal sending disposition, including failures, bounces, complaints, and unsubscribes, but excluding skipped recipients; it does not mean delivered.
